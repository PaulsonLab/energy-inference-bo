# Task 01 smoke results

Configuration: 3 fitting seeds (`0, 1, 2`), residual sample sizes `20, 50, 100, 200, 500`, a 20,000-sample held-out log-score set, 64-point Gauss–Hermite residual normalization, and CPU-only double precision. Generated by `python -m energy_bo.experiments.run_task01`.

## Eight completion questions

1. **Does matched-moment shape change EI decisions?** Yes in the predetermined tail-sensitive scenario: oracle-mixture EI selects `x=0.250` while Gaussian EI selects `x=0.750`; Spearman correlation is `0.53116`, top-5% overlap is `0.000`, and the maximum EI error is `0.012344`. The two non-tail scenarios are retained in the CSV rather than searched for favorable outcomes.
2. **Does the residual energy beat fitted Gaussian location/scale?** It beats both held-out log score and KL at sample sizes `[20, 50, 100, 200, 500]`. The three-seed means are below.

| n | Gaussian MLE log score | EBM log score | Gaussian MLE KL | EBM KL |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | -1.55891 | -1.42100 | 0.15801 | 0.01868 |
| 50 | -1.45040 | -1.41692 | 0.04839 | 0.01418 |
| 100 | -1.42974 | -1.41507 | 0.02818 | 0.01304 |
| 200 | -1.42067 | -1.41337 | 0.01843 | 0.01067 |
| 500 | -1.41537 | -1.40679 | 0.01326 | 0.00446 |

3. **Are gains merely moment shifts?** No. The largest fitted EBM `|mean| + |variance - 1|` over all smoke fits is `0.26597`, so finite-sample moment movement is visible; however, the EBM still beats the fitted location/scale Gaussian (not merely fixed N(0,1)) on both log score and KL at every sample size in answer 2.
4. **Does M=1 augmentation match expected utility?** The normalized grid maximum error is `1.110e-15`, with identical modes at `0.250`.
5. **Do M=2 and M=4 follow power sharpening?** Maximum normalized grid errors are `1.554e-15` and `3.331e-15` respectively; the uniform-prior mode remains unchanged.
6. **How quickly does ESS deteriorate?** With 30,000 particles, ESS fractions for M=1/2/4 are `0.2165`, `0.0432`, and `0.0013`. Raw weights were used, so zero-weight outcomes and degeneracy are visible rather than masked by log-energy smoothing.
7. **Does exact-GP q=1 augmentation reproduce EI/LogEI?** Yes: analytic-vs-quadrature EI max discrepancy `9.714e-17`, augmented-vs-EI normalized marginal discrepancy `1.599e-14`, and all M=1 maximizers are `x=0.303`. The uniform-prior M=2 marginal error is `3.908e-14`.
8. **Recommended next stage:** **both**. Gate 1 (oracle value) is `True`, Gate 2 (learnability beyond fitted Gaussian) is `True`, Gate 3 (q=1 identity) is `True`, and the M=4 simple-IS degeneracy condition is `True`. This does not implement Task 02.
